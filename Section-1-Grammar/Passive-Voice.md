## 1. Impersonal Passive

The Impersonal Passive is used to report information, opinions, or beliefs without emphasizing who said or reported it.

**Structure:**

`It + be + past participle + that + clause`

**Examples:**

- It is believed that the tests produce false positives.  
  Acredita-se que os testes produzem falsos positivos.

- It was reported that the system had security issues.  
  Foi relatado que o sistema tinha problemas de segurança.

## 2. Personal Passive

The Personal Passive places emphasis on the subject of the sentence.

**Structure:**

`Subject + passive reporting verb + to-infinitive`

**Examples:**

- The tests are thought to produce false positives.  
  Acredita-se que os testes produzem falsos positivos.

- The system is expected to improve performance.  
  Espera-se que o sistema melhore o desempenho.

## 3. Common Reporting Verbs

- Believe — acreditar
- Expect — esperar
- Find — constatar
- Know — saber
- Report — relatar
- Say — dizer
- Think — pensar
- Understand — compreender
